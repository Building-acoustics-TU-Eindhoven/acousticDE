# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to creating a welcoming, safe, inclusive, and fair environment for everyone in our community.

We strive to build a community that recognizes and respects the dignity, rights, and contributions of every individual, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophical or religious beliefs, national or social background, socioeconomic status, educational background, or any other personal characteristic or status. The same privileges of participation are extended to everyone who participates in good faith and in accordance with this Covenant.

## Expected and unacceptable Behaviors

We recognize that social norms and communication styles can vary across cultures, backgrounds, and languages. We also understand that our words and actions may sometimes be interpreted differently from how we intended them. Despite these differences, we share a responsibility to contribute positively to our community and treat one another with consideration. We encourage everyone to:

1. Respect the **purpose, activities, and practices of our community**, including the ways in which we interact and collaborate.
2. Communicate with others in a **kind, honest, and respectful** manner.
3. Welcome and respect **different perspectives, opinions, and experiences**.
4. Take **responsibility for our actions**, decisions, and contributions.
5. Give and receive **constructive feedback** openly and respectfully.
6. Make a genuine effort to **acknowledge, address, and repair harm** when it occurs.
7. Act in other ways that support the **safety, inclusion, and overall well-being of our community**.

The following behaviors are not permitted within our community. This includes engaging in, threatening, encouraging, or promoting any of these behaviors.

1. **Harassment.** Ignoring clearly stated boundaries or continuing unwanted personal attention after someone has asked for it to stop.
2. **Personal attacks.** Making insulting, degrading, or derogatory remarks about an individual or group.
3. **Stereotyping and discrimination.** Making assumptions about someone's personality, abilities, or behavior based on their identity or personal characteristics.
4. **Inappropriate sexual behavior.** Engaging in sexualized or overly intimate conduct that is inappropriate for the context or purpose of the community.
5. **Breaches of confidentiality.** Disclosing, distributing, or using another person's private or personal information without their consent.
6. **Threats and endangerment.** Causing, threatening, or encouraging violence or other forms of harm against an individual or group.
7. **Other harmful conduct.** Engaging in any behavior that compromises the safety, trust, or well-being of the community.

## Reporting an issue

Disagreements and misunderstandings can arise even when community members have good intentions and are trying to work together. Not every conflict constitutes a violation of this Code of Conduct. However, the principles outlined here are intended to encourage respectful interactions, prevent unnecessary conflict, and minimize harm.

If you witness or experience a potential violation, we encourage you to report it as soon as possible. To submit a report, **create an issue in the project repository with the title "Code of Conduct Violation: [Brief Description]".** All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly, impartially, and fairly. Community leaders are responsible for protecting the privacy and security of anyone who reports an incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines when this Code of Conduct is violated. Depending on the seriousness of a violation, some steps may be skipped when a stronger response is necessary.

### 1. Warning

**When it applies:** A person commits an isolated violation or engages in a series of related minor incidents.

**Response:** A private, written warning explaining the concern and the behavior that needs to change will be issued. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Temporary ban

**When it applies:** A person repeats behavior for which they have already received a warning, or commits a more serious violation for the first time.

**Response:** A private, written warning and impose a temporary restriction on participation will be issued. This restriction provides time for those involved to process the incident and emphasizes the seriousness of the behavior. Restrictions may apply to specific communication channels, activities, or interactions with particular community members.

### 3. Permanent ban

**When it applies:** A person repeatedly violates the Code of Conduct despite previous interventions, or commits a sufficiently serious violation that would pose an unacceptable risk to the community.

**Response:** The person's access to community spaces, tools, activities, and communication channels is permanently revoked Permanent removal should generally be reserved for cases where the seriousness of the incident or the failure of previous interventions makes continued participation inappropriate. Any such decision should be supported by clear reasoning and a careful assessment of community safety.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public or other spaces. Examples of representing our community include using an official email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.
